THE ANATOMY OF A REPORTCOA / FIELD NOTES
CoACertificate of analysis
01 Batch identification +

Match the product and batch identifier on the report to the product in your hands.

02 Cannabinoid profile +

Look for the compounds measured, their amounts, and the units used to report them.

03 Tests & laboratory +

Check the named laboratory, test date and scope. A report only describes the sample and tests it covers.

GOOD QUESTIONS03 / STRAIGHTFORWARD ANSWERS

A little clarity.

What is in the collection?+

The new EU preview focuses on three hemp and CBD categories: Vapes, Pre-Rolls and Edibles, including gummies. Images illustrate the proposed direction; final product specifications and availability will follow.

Can I order from the EU yet?+

This EU edition currently lets you explore the range and save products to a personal selection. EU prices, delivery and checkout are not yet available. Contact the EHF team for current product enquiries.

Where can I find a lab report?+

Our lab-report guide explains what to look for. You can also visit EHF’s existing certificates page. Check that any report matches the exact product and batch you are considering.

Read the guide
How does my saved selection work?+

Save products as you browse, then open Selection in the navigation. Your list is stored only in this browser. You can remove items or download the list; saving an item does not place an order.

How can I contact Exclusive Hemp Farms?+

Use the contact page on the main EHF website to reach the team.

Contact the EHF team
