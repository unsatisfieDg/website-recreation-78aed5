ABOUT THIS PREVIEW

A LITTLE
transparency.

What this preview stores

Your saved product selection and motion preference are stored locally in this browser. The landing sound toggle remembers your ambience preference. Water, birds and distant park chatter play only during the entrance, subject to browser playback permission. The original theme song has been removed. The playlist button opens a Spotify player only when you choose it. Its frame stays connected during internal navigation and when minimized. Playback is controlled inside Spotify’s player. The site does not create an account, process an order, or collect payment details.

What leaves your browser

Website files, images, videos and landing ambience are served by the host. Opening the playlist connects your browser to Spotify and loads its embedded player. Spotify handles its own playback, account features and data practices; see Spotify’s privacy policy. You can also open the playlist directly using its Open in Spotify link. The site adds no analytics, advertising trackers, newsletter submissions or third-party font embeds. The host may keep its own request logs.

Links to the main EHF website

Links to product information, certificates and contact details open the main Exclusive Hemp Farms website. That website operates separately and has its own data practices.

Your controls

You can remove products through Selection, change motion using the footer control, or clear this preview’s saved settings below.

Before the EU store launches

This explains the current preview only. The final EU business details, privacy policy, terms, delivery and returns information must be supplied and reviewed before commerce services are connected.
